How one sound is made

Each voice follows the path a sample took through the PlayStation 2's sound chip. Nothing is stored: every number below is worked out from the sound's name and the world seed.

  1. Hash the nameWorld seed, then category, sound, voice and parameter slot, each hashed from the one above.
  2. Write a source sampleAn FM tone, wavetable, detuned ensemble loop, filtered noise sweep or pitch-drop sine. Filter sweeps are baked into the sample, because the chip has no filter.
  3. Squeeze it through ADPCM28 samples become 16 bytes, then get decoded again, which adds the console's grain.
  4. Play it as a voiceA pitch counter reads the sample at 48 kHz through 4-point Gaussian smoothing, shaped by an ADSR envelope.
  5. Place itFront voices are panned. Rear voices get a negative right volume, which a surround decoder steers behind the listener.
  6. Add the effect coresThree or four voices are summed with a half-rate reverb and a ping-pong echo.
